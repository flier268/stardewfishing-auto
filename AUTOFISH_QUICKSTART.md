# Auto-Fishing Quick Start Guide

## What is Auto-Fishing?

Auto-fishing is an AI-powered feature that automatically controls the fishing minigame for you. It's perfect for:
- Learning how the fishing minigame works
- Accessibility support
- Relaxed gameplay
- AFK fishing sessions

## How to Use (3 Easy Steps)

### Step 1: Start Fishing Normally
1. Cast your fishing rod as usual
2. Wait for a fish to bite
3. The minigame will start automatically

### Step 2: Watch the Auto-Fish Work
- **Look at the top-left corner** of the screen
- You'll see: **"Auto: ON (A)"** in green
- The AI is now controlling the bobber for you!
- Just sit back and watch

### Step 3: Toggle On/Off (Optional)
- **Press the `A` key** at any time to turn auto-fishing on or off
- Green = Auto is ON
- Red = Auto is OFF
- You can switch back and forth whenever you want!

## Tips & Tricks

1. **Manual Override**: You can click anytime to manually control, even with auto-fishing on
2. **Works with Chests**: The AI will try to catch treasure chests while keeping the fish
3. **Difficulty Adaptive**: Works better with larger bobber bars and easier fish
4. **Sound Feedback**: Listen for the sound when toggling (success sound = ON, dwop = OFF)

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Auto-fishing isn't working!**
- Make sure you see "Auto: ON" in green at the top-left
- Try pressing `A` to toggle it

**Q: The AI keeps losing the fish!**
- Some legendary/difficult fish are very hard, even for AI
- Try using better fishing rods to increase bar size
- You can manually help by clicking when needed

**Q: Can I change the toggle key from 'A'?**
- Currently not configurable, but future updates may add this

**Q: Does it work in multiplayer?**
- Yes! It's purely client-side

## Default Settings

- **Default State**: Auto-fishing is **ENABLED** when minigame starts
- **Toggle Key**: `A` key
- **Performance**: No lag, runs smoothly
